About LVL Carts

A St. George family
raising the floor.

At LVL Carts, we pride ourselves on being St. George's premier destination for quality golf carts. Our roots run deep in the Southern Utah community as a locally owned and independently operated family business. Having raised our children here, we are passionate about giving back and ensuring our neighbors receive unparalleled service and top-quality products.

Our specialization in Atlas Carts and Royal EVs ensures that our customers have access to the latest innovations and the most reliable vehicles in the market. Whether you're navigating the golf course, exploring your neighborhood, or seeking an eco-friendly transportation alternative, our curated selection promises style and performance.

Customer satisfaction is at the heart of everything we do. From the moment you step into our showroom to the after-sales support, our dedicated team is committed to exceeding your expectations.
